# Earmatch 0.1.2

- Earmatch now asks for its two permissions before anything else. Until Accessibility and Screen Recording are both allowed, its window shows only the permissions screen, the overlays and hotkeys stay off, and the menu bar menu only offers to allow them, open Earmatch or quit. It starts on its own a moment after both are allowed, and locks again if one is turned off.
- The download opens on a designed window: drag Earmatch onto the Applications folder, with Earmatch's icon on the disk.
